What it does

Reads EPUBs beautifully

Paginated rendering with five themes — light, dark, sepia, forest, ocean. Pick your typeface, adjust size and spacing. The text reflows to fit.

Works offline

Everything lives on your device. Open the app, pick a book, read. No Wi-Fi, no login, no fuss.

Highlights & bookmarks

Select text, highlight it, find it later. Bookmark pages to return to. Your annotations travel with the book.

Sync on your terms

Optional PocketBase server you can self-host. Sync books, progress, highlights, and shelves across devices. Or don't — the app works fine alone.

Shelves

Organize your library into named collections.

Discover

Browse Open Library to find your next read.

Multiple windows

Open several books at once on macOS.
