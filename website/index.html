Why Moku?

📖

Beautiful Reading

Paginated EPUB renderer with multiple themes — light, dark, sepia, forest, ocean. Customize fonts, sizes, and spacing.

✈️

Offline First

Works completely offline. Your books live on your device. No account, no internet connection required to read.

🔄

Self-Hostable Sync

Optional PocketBase sync server. Host your own, or use ours. Sync books, reading progress, highlights, and shelves.

🔖

Highlights & Bookmarks

Highlight passages and add bookmarks while you read. All your annotations stay organized and easily accessible.

📚

Organize with Shelves

Create custom shelves to organize your library. Sort by title, author, or recently read.

🔍

Discover Books

Browse and discover books from Open Library. Find your next read without leaving the app.

Free & Open Source

Moku is completely open source. Inspect the code, contribute features, or host your own sync server.
